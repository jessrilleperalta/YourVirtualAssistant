About

Jessrille is a Bachelor of Arts in International Studies student and Dean’s Lister at Ateneo de Zamboanga University, graduating with Cum Laude honors. She has developed hands-on expertise in project management, member engagement, and financial administration through leadership roles with Ateneans 4 Palestine and the International Studies Organization. Her professional experience includes serving as a Regional Associate Intern for the European Chamber of Commerce of the Philippines, where she supported event execution, market research, and social media management.
